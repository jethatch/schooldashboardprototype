About the data

This draft explorer uses only public data. It describes areas and the groups of people who live there (2020 Census blocks), not individual families or students, and it makes no recommendations.

  • Where people live: 2020 Census blocks in the four districts; household population (people in group quarters such as prisons, nursing homes and college dorms are left out). Children are under 18. Block counts carry Census privacy noise: look at areas, not single blocks.
  • Income: American Community Survey 2020–2024 (table B17024), by tract, spread to blocks. Every block in a tract shares its tract's income mix, so income gaps are conservative.
  • Schools: Delaware Department of Education directory and enrollment; every public school statewide is a possible destination. Charter, magnet, vo-tech and choice-program groupings follow the earlier Wilmington-area report. Special programs (referral only) are left out.
  • Zoned school: the Department of Education's 2026–27 attendance zones, grade by grade.
  • Travel times: computed on OpenStreetMap roads: walking at a child's pace (4 km/h), driving without traffic. Times start from each block (larger blocks from points along their roads). Walking times are long and skewed, so read medians and shares beside averages.
  • Zoning detour: extra time to the zoned school compared with the nearest regular school in the same district. Extra minutes to choose: nearest school of a type minus the zoned school; negative means the choice is closer.
  • Charters within 5 miles: charters whose 5-mile admission-preference radius (straight line) covers the block; potential priority only, for charters that use it.
  • Not included: traffic, road safety, school buses, transit, and admission rules (applications, lotteries, preferences, auditions).
